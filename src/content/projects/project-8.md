## Overview

RISE (Robotics & Intelligent Systems Expo) is a student‑led expo at Purdue that brings together students, researchers, and companies for live robotics demos, student project showcases, research talks, and industry panels. A previous edition drew **500+ attendees** and **100+ participants** from five universities.

I joined as **Director of Outreach** and later became the **Director**. I am currently a Director. I helped build the organizing team from the ground up and scale the expo by bringing more research labs, robotics companies, and student teams into the program. I led outreach, met directly with founders to figure out what would make exhibiting worthwhile for them, and helped student teams work through what they needed to demo their projects.

Our team raised **$6,000** to put on the event, with sponsorship from **Purdue ICON** and **Purdue’s Department of Industrial Engineering**.
