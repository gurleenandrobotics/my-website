## Overview

**🏆 1st Place — Major League Hacking × Innovate Her Hackathon (2025)**

Built in a 24-hour hackathon sprint, this web application provides **real-time fire evacuation guidance and live location tracking** to help people navigate emergencies safely. It was one of two back-to-back #1 wins at MLH × Innovate Her.

## The Problem

During building evacuations, occupants often lack:
- Real-time awareness of fire spread and blocked exits.
- A way to share their live location with emergency responders or teammates.

## What We Built

- **Live Location Tracking:** Users share GPS location in real time, visible on a live map.
- **Fire Spread Visualization:** Dynamic map overlay showing reported fire zones and safe egress routes.
- **Evacuation Route Engine:** Automatically suggests the nearest safe exit based on current fire data.
- **Responsive UI:** Mobile-first design built in React and styled with Figma mockups.

## Outcome

Won **1st place** at the MLH × Innovate Her hackathon. The judges highlighted the real-world impact potential, the clean UI, and how quickly the team shipped a working prototype.
